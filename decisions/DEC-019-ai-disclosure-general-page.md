# DEC-019 — Transparency on the use of AI: a general disclosure, no per-content label

Status: DECIDED

Question: how does QN declare its use of AI?

Options:
A. A stable label on every article, event and card (the prudent default of Rounds 1–2).
B. **CHOSEN.** A general disclosure on an editorial-methodology page reachable from the
   institutional sections of the site; no label per content or per Conversation card,
   unless a legal obligation requires one.

Claude recommendation: A as the default until the legal opinion answers; the owner
chooses B, with adaptation to any obligation per single content.

ChatGPT recommendation: the same.

## Rule

B. The page describes the prevalent use of primary and official sources, the role of
the two AIs (DEC-015), the routing of ambiguous cases to manual review, the sampling of
published content and the report-and-correction procedure (DEC-020). If a legal opinion
identifies disclosure obligations per single content, QN adopts the requirement actually
necessary. This is a declared risk assumption of the owner (DEC-014, "Legal verifications
and the launch"); the general disclosure closes the editorial preference, not the
possible obligation per content.

Impact: visible legal question (art. 50(4) AI Act; L. 132/2025).

Blocks: nothing (this entry unblocks the methodology page and the content layout).

owner confirmation) · Affected: site (methodology page), content layout.
