# DEC-020 — Corrections: direct edit with internal traceability, no public note and no SLA

Status: DECIDED
Supersedes in part: DEC-103 (unchanged and not reopened; the clause "every fix lands in
the gazetteer, the venue registry or the source registry, never in the individual item",
limited to content corrections and rectifications concerning persons).

Question: how are errors in published content corrected?

Options: direct edit with internal traceability (**CHOSEN**); public correction note and
public log with a 48-hour SLA (Round 2 mitigation); no correction policy.

Claude recommendation: Round 2 proposed a 48-hour SLA and a public log as mitigations;
the owner excludes both. Visible legal question: publication of a rectification
requested by the person concerned (art. 8 L. 47/1948 where applicable; a conventional
procedure otherwise) and de-indexing — a silent edit may not satisfy such a request.

ChatGPT recommendation: the same.

## Rule

- An identified error is corrected directly in the published content. No correction note
  and no public history, as the general rule.
- The system keeps internally the previous version, the change, the date, the reason, the
  origin of the report and any manual intervention.
- No KPI and no SLA on corrections.
- Potentially harmful content may be suspended while awaiting correction.
- Geographic fixes still land in the registries (DEC-103, intact clause); this entry
  concerns the correction of the content itself.
- This is a declared risk assumption of the owner; a rectification procedure on request
  is defined after the legal opinion, and the rule is modified if the opinion finds an
  incompatible obligation (DEC-014, "Legal verifications and the launch").

Impact: per-item versioning; suspension fields; a rectification-on-request procedure to
be defined after the opinion.

Blocks: nothing (this entry unblocks the versioning data model).
