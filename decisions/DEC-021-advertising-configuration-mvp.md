# DEC-021 — Advertising configuration of the MVP: commercial page, free campaigns, political advertising inactive

Status: DECIDED

Question: which advertising configuration does the MVP launch with?

Options: a commercial page, a few unobtrusive blocks filled by free campaigns, political
advertising admitted in principle but inactive (**CHOSEN**); a public price list with a
public advertisers' register and a `commercial_conflict` field (Rounds 1–2); no
advertising surface at launch.

Claude recommendation: Rounds 1–2 excluded political advertising from the MVP and
proposed a public register and `commercial_conflict`; the owner excludes them at
launch. Visible legal questions: advertising labelling under the Codice del consumo and
art. 7 of the IAP code, which applies to the free campaigns too — they are advertising
by nature and presentation even without consideration (it is not established that their
gratuity must be declared publicly); ROC and fiscal obligations at the first revenue;
Reg. (EU) 2024/900 and electoral law before the first political sale.

ChatGPT recommendation: the same.

## Rule

- At launch: a commercial page (the possibility of promoting a business, the distinction
  between advertising and editorial content, a contact). Prices, packages and structure
  within the following six months. No public price list, no public register, no
  conflict-management software, no discount thresholds, no minimum number of offers and
  no hour cap at launch.
- Before the first sale: space, duration, geographic perimeter, price, advertising
  label, absence of editorial influence.
- A few unobtrusive blocks; AdSense not on day one. Spaces filled by free campaigns of
  external businesses selected offline, marked as advertising, in a standard format,
  without editorial content or influence, of limited duration, recorded as free, not
  counted in the first-payment KPI (DEC-017), with a discreet link to the commercial
  page.
- Political advertising admitted in principle but inactive. A concrete request stays
  suspended until legal verification and a minimum policy exist; only advertising with
  a recognisable principal, no editorial influence, no use of newsletter data for
  political profiling. It requires neither a partner nor a second responsible person
  and does not block the launch (DEC-014, "Legal verifications and the launch").

Blocks: nothing (this entry unblocks the site's advertising blocks and the commercial
page).
