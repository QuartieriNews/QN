# DEC-105 — Named participants in the sources study

Status: DECIDED (17 September 2026)

Supersedes in part: DEC-102 (DECIDED, August 2026) — only the person-name clause the
sources study built on top of it. DEC-102's own rules stand unchanged: manual URL
collection, credit and link back, no redistribution of source data as a dataset,
removal requests honoured via the published contact address.

Rule: no automatic anonymisation of the people a news item is about. Names of
assessori, consiglieri, funzionari, cittadini, comitato representatives, professionals
and firms — ditte individuali included — are kept when they are pertinent to the fact.
Name, role and organisation are what let a reader understand responsibility and the
dynamics of a quartiere; removing them costs the reader exactly that. Names already
replaced by a generic formula are restored, each verified against its source. An
omission is allowed only for a concrete reason in the content, stated row by row; the
mere fact that a string is a person's name is not a reason.

Two boundaries stay in force and are not touched by this entry:

1. **The dataset boundary (DEC-102, DEC-104).** Per-item source data still does not
   enter the public repository; it is delivered on Drive. This entry changes what the
   fields contain, not where they live.
2. **The session boundary (D7).** Anything outside the formally open sitting —
   suspensions included — stays out of the analysis and out of the news, names and all.
   Openings, suspensions, resumptions and closures are the markers. A speaker saying
   "fuori verbale" does not suspend a sitting, so what is said inside an open sitting is
   in scope under the rule above.

Consequence for territory, decided with this entry: the cited place yields both the
Municipio and the quartiere, checked for geographic correspondence, with the
common-use quartiere kept distinct from any administrative subdivision. A deduced
attribution is declared as deduced; ambiguous cases and cases spanning more than one
territory stay explicit rather than being forced to a single value.
